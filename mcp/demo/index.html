Pairing

Obala na naslovima, Kej u čitanju

Made in Prototypo for Školjka production. Both cuts start from Prototypo Grotesk, then split like CSTM’s display/text optical sizes and Commercial Type’s Graphik-weight stack — not their outlines. Obala is the headline size: heavier, slightly wide, higher x-height, tighter, a bit more contrast, baked Q_alt. Kej is the reading size: lower contrast, modest aperture so G stays a G, extra spacing, č ć š ž đ kept clear at 16–18px.
